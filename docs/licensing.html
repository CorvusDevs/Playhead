Playhead

Licensing and relinking materials

Playhead includes FFmpeg under LGPL 2.1 or later, MediaInfoLib under BSD 2-Clause, and ZenLib under the Zlib licence.

For every shipped version, this page publishes the exact FFmpeg source archive, Playhead object files for macOS and iOS, and the build script and configure flags used to produce that version. Those materials let recipients relink Playhead with their own compatible FFmpeg build.
